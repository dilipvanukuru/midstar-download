Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short

  • Your documents, their index and your chats stay on your PC. The index and chats are encrypted there.
  • Mid Star has no servers that receive your documents or questions, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • To search and answer, the app sends text to the AI service you choose, with your key. That's the only place your content goes.

What stays on your PC

Your documents, the search index built from them, your chats and answers, your settings and API keys, your profile picture and the Pro licence file are kept in the app's data folder on your PC (%APPDATA%\Mid Star Chatbot). The index, answers, chat titles and conversation memory are encrypted with a key that Windows protects for your user account. Uninstalling the app asks whether to delete this folder too; your own document files are never deleted.

Everything the app sends, and to whom

Each AI service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy and data terms, which you accept when you create your key. Several of them offer settings to stop your data being used for training; check your account with them.

What we never collect

  • Your documents, questions, answers or chats.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry of any kind. The Usage page in the app is worked out on your PC and stays there.
  • Your name, email or payment details, except what you choose to put in a feedback message. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, not by us.

Payments

When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to process the payment, as the seller of record. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy. We see the order (your name, email and what you bought) in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard, and use it only to support your order and licence.

This website

This page and the download are hosted on GitHub, which records visits and downloads as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The page uses Google Fonts. It sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a document, a chat or all your data at any time from inside the app, or by deleting its data folder.
  • Choose an AI service that runs on your PC for searching, or set sensitive documents to keywords only, so their text isn't sent anywhere.
  • Turn off the daily update check by setting MIDSTAR_UPDATE_CHECK=0.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you (feedback messages and orders), send feedback from the app or reply to your receipt.

Changes

If the app starts sending anything new, we'll update this page before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
