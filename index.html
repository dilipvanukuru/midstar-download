Install

  1. Run MidStarSetup.exe. It installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator rights needed.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. On first launch, choose your AI services and paste your API key. Cohere does everything with one key from dashboard.cohere.com/api-keys.
  4. Add your documents in Settings → Documents, or attach them from the question box.

Installing over an earlier version keeps your library, keys and conversations.
